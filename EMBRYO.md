# Embryo Rack Map

Embryo Rack Map is Embryo-Zero's fork of [netbox](https://github.com/netbox-community/netbox) (f766cc5, commit f766cc5). It does data-center inventory: racks, machines, cables, IPs (DCIM/IPAM).

- Our changes live on the `embryo` branch. The original license files (LICENSE.txt, NOTICE) are unchanged.
- Legal notes: LEGAL.md. Dated list of our changes: CHANGES-EMBRYO.md.
